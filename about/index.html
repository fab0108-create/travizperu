How a private trip is arranged

You tell me the dates, the pace, and what you actually care about seeing. Within 24 hours I send a proposal with hotels, guiding, and logistics, including Machu Picchu tickets when that day is part of the journey. There is no public rate card. The trip is quoted for the way you want to travel.

The places I plan most often are luxury Machu Picchu tours, private Sacred Valley tours, a private Lima guide, and private Lake Titicaca tours. For timing, read how many days in Peru and whether Peru is safe to visit in 2026.
