Combining Peru with Argentina or Brazil

Iguazú Falls is on the border between Argentina and Brazil. Private, tailor-made journeys can include the falls, Buenos Aires or Rio de Janeiro on their own, or added before or after Peru.

Citizens of the United States, Canada and Australia need a Brazilian e-visa (VIVIS), in effect since 10 April 2025. The application is made only through VFS Global, and the official guidance is to apply before buying flights. Sources: the Brazilian Ministry of Foreign Affairs consulate in Los Angeles, the consulate in Atlanta, and the U.S. Embassy in Brazil.

For Argentina and Costa Rica, check entry rules with the official consulate for your passport.

These places can be visited on their own, or added before or after Peru. Costa Rica also has its own page, and Peru with Costa Rica is one private journey.
