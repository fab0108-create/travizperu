Best Time to Visit Peru: Month by Month, Region by Region

Photo: Jorge Láscar, CC BY 2.0. Resized for this page.

By Francisco Arriz Barrantes · TravizPeru · Updated 30 October 2026

Peru has no single "best month". The Andes, the coast and the Amazon follow different patterns, so the right time depends on where you go and what matters most to you: clear skies, fewer visitors or milder nights. This guide uses Peru's official weather service (SENAMHI) and official park notices.

The short answer

  • Andes (Cusco, Sacred Valley, Machu Picchu): the driest months are roughly May to September; the rainy season runs roughly December to March, wettest in January–February. April and October are shoulder months.
  • Lima and the coast: almost no rain all year. Summer (about December–April) is sunny and warm; June–September is the grey, humid "garúa" season, cool but rarely wet (SENAMHI Lima normals: July–August highs around 18–19 °C).
  • Amazon: humid and rainy all year; there is no truly dry month.

Month-by-month in the Andes

  • January–February: wettest months. Trails and roads can be affected by rain. The classic Inca Trail network closes every February for maintenance (SERNANP; in 2026 it reopened on 1 March). Machu Picchu itself stays open.
  • March–April: rains taper off; green landscapes.
  • May: dry and green — one of our favorite months.
  • June–August: driest and clearest, but nights are cold (frost season in the high Andes) and it is the busiest period.
  • September–October: still largely dry, warmer nights, fewer visitors than mid-year.
  • November–December: rains begin to build.

What this means for a 45+ traveler

Cold nights matter more than a few millimetres of rain: we choose hotels with good heating and plan Machu Picchu early or late in the day depending on the season. Whatever the month, we follow the same altitude rule: fly Lima–Cusco and go first to the Sacred Valley (lower), and leave Cusco city for later. See the altitude guide.

Weather is not the climate

Monthly averages are a guide, not a forecast. For current conditions check SENAMHI, and our pages on El Niño and train status.

FAQ

What is the best month to visit Machu Picchu?

May to September are the driest; May and September balance dry weather with fewer visitors than June–August.

When is the rainy season in Peru?

In the Andes roughly December to March, wettest in January and February. Lima gets almost no rain.

Is the Inca Trail closed in February?

Yes, the Inca Trail network closes every February for maintenance (SERNANP). Machu Picchu remains open.

Is Lima nice in July?

It is cool, grey and humid but rarely rainy. For sunshine, visit Lima between December and April.

Is it cold in Cusco in June and July?

Days are usually pleasant and sunny, but nights can drop near freezing.

Can I visit Peru in the rainy season?

Yes. With flexible timing and private transfers, many travelers enjoy green landscapes and fewer visitors.
