Is El Niño a risk for your Peru trip?

Photo: Pedro Szekely, CC BY-SA 2.0. Resized for this page.

By Francisco Arriz Barrantes · TravizPeru · Updated 27 October 2026 · Leer en español

There is a lot of talk about a very intense El Niño. It is a large ocean event, but its effect inside Peru varies by region, and official forecasts point mainly to the north and central coast. Here is what sources say as of October 27, 2026.

What is happening

ENFEN keeps a Coastal El Niño Alert. Its Official Statement No. 18-2026 (Oct 16, 2026) says warming off Peru will most likely stay extraordinary through January 2027, then ease to strong until early autumn 2027.

NOAA's Climate Prediction Center (Oct 8, 2026) expects a strong-to-very strong El Niño through January–March 2027 (greater than 83%) and gives an 83% chance of a historic-strength event in October–December. It notes impacts are more likely, "though not guaranteed".

Where it would be felt most

ENFEN expects above-normal rain on the north coast, central coast and western northern highlands, high river flows from November in northern rivers and from December in Lima's Rímac and Chillón, and very high coastal temperatures. SENAMHI (Oct 22, 2026) projects north-coast rain to intensify from November.

So trips to the north coast from November 2026 and through the 2027 summer need the most flexibility.

Cusco, Machu Picchu and the south

For the southern highlands and the Amazon, ENFEN expects normal to below-normal rain and a below-normal Vilcanota river. This is not a promise of good weather: the highland rainy season happens every year. As of today, PeruRail's notices report no rain-related suspensions.

The Inca Trail closed for all of February 2026 for annual maintenance and reopened March 1 (SERNANP, Ministry of Culture). 2027 dates should be confirmed once announced.

FAQ

Should travelers cancel a Peru trip because of El Niño?

Official forecasts don't frame it that way. Main impacts are expected on the north and central coast; each trip is reviewed by region and dates.

Will Machu Picchu close because of El Niño?

No official source we reviewed announces that. ENFEN expects normal to below-normal rain in the southern highlands.

When is the most sensitive period on the north coast?

According to ENFEN and SENAMHI, from November 2026 and through the 2027 summer.

Where can I follow updates?

ENFEN's next statement is October 30, 2026; NOAA's next update is November 12, 2026.
