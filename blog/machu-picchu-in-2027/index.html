Machu Picchu in 2027: how to plan and when to book

You can plan Machu Picchu for 2027 now, and you cannot yet buy the ticket against a published opening day. The Ministry of Culture has set the daily caps. It has not published the date when 2027 tickets go on sale. The only official online sale site is tuboleto.cultura.pe. We request tickets as soon as you confirm, subject to availability.

There is no published sale date

On 31 October 2026 the Ministry signed two resolutions. Resolución Ministerial N.° 408-2026-MC sets how many people may enter the Llaqta in 2027. Resolución Ministerial N.° 407-2026-MC changes the alternate-route rules from 1 January 2027, and it tells the Cusco office to configure those hours on Tu Boleto before ticket sales for 2027 begin. Neither resolution states the opening day. El Peruano published N.° 407-2026-MC on 1 November 2026. The text is dated San Borja, 31 October 2026.

On 14 October 2026 the minister told TVPerú that buying up to a year ahead would be possible very soon, and he used a July visit as the example. That is a statement of intent. It is not a sale date, and we have not found a later official notice that turns it into one. As of 24 November 2026, no official 2027 sale date is published. Check tuboleto.cultura.pe for the dates actually on the calendar. How buying worked in 2026, including the September trial, is in the ticket guide. A 2026 screen is not a 2027 promise.

Where the ticket is sold

The only official online sale site is tuboleto.cultura.pe. Resolución Ministerial N.° 408-2026-MC also allows in-person sale at the authorized offices of the Dirección Desconcentrada de Cultura de Cusco. The Ministry's service page asks for an identity document (DNI, carné de extranjería or passport) and an email to use the platform. The ticket is for one route and one entry hour. The 22 September 2026 notice said the ticket is issued in the visitor's name once payment is confirmed.

That notice opened a 30-day trial of direct sale. During the trial the buyer had a maximum of 15 minutes to pay, and the Ministry said it would watch whether that window should be longer or shorter. We have not found a later notice that freezes a different window for 2027. The service page still describes an older reserve-then-pay sequence. Read the screen on the day you buy. We do not use any other website. We request tickets as soon as you confirm, subject to availability. We do not guarantee a ticket, a circuit or an hour.

How many people can enter in 2027

Resolución Ministerial N.° 408-2026-MC sets 5,600 visitors a day on 147 dates: 1 to 3 January 2027, 25 to 28 March 2027, 19 June to 2 November 2027, and 29 to 31 December 2027. Every other day in 2027 the cap is 4,500. The same resolution approves a dynamic cap by circuit, route, sales channel and entry hour. The annex is the detail. The calendar on Tu Boleto is what is actually for sale. A day that is not full can still have one route sold out.

UNESCO lists the Historic Sanctuary at 2,430 m, in tropical mountain forest, and treats visitor numbers as something the site has to manage. SERNANP manages the sanctuary as a protected area. The citadel ticket is the Ministry's, on Tu Boleto. The Inca Trail is a different permit: SERNANP says visits on that network are made with an authorized operator. We have not found a published 2027 opening date for the Trail either.

Circuits and routes

Since 1 June 2024 the official circuit list has been three circuits and ten routes. The ticket you buy is one of these, for one hour:

  • Circuit 1, Panoramic. Route 1-A is Machu Picchu Mountain. Route 1-B is the upper terrace. Routes 1-C (Intipunku) and 1-D (the Inca Bridge) are marked on that page as high season only.
  • Circuit 2, Classic. Route 2-A is the designed route. Route 2-B is the lower terrace. When the visit is the citadel itself, this is the circuit we look at first.
  • Circuit 3, Royalty. Route 3-A is Huayna Picchu (Montaña Waynapicchu). Route 3-B is the designed route of the same circuit, without the summit. Routes 3-C (the Great Cavern) and 3-D (Huchuy Picchu) are marked as high season only. 3-A and 3-B are different tickets.

From 1 January 2027, Resolución Ministerial N.° 407-2026-MC rewrites the alternate-route rules. The alternate routes are 1-A, 1-C, 1-D, 3-A and 3-C. They are voluntary, they need the matching ticket, and the regulation says the visitor should be in good physical condition and register at the control point. Entry to Machu Picchu Mountain, Huayna Picchu and the Great Cavern is until 10:00. The latest departure from those high points is 12:00, and the latest return from Machu Picchu Mountain is 14:00. The Great Cavern is enabled in high season. The circuits page and the resolution do not describe every route in the same words. For your date, the record that matters is Tu Boleto. We do not guarantee a circuit.

The Machu Picchu page is the visit itself. The private tour is that day with your own guide, the train and the entry requested together.

Trains

SERNANP lists a commercial train from Cusco and from Ollantaytambo to Machu Picchu Pueblo, the town also called Aguas Calientes, and then the visit continues to the citadel. PeruRail and Inca Rail both carry passengers on that line. For most private trips the platform is Ollantaytambo, in the Sacred Valley, not Cusco. The hour on the citadel ticket and the arrival of the train have to agree. For an early gate, we sleep in the town the night before. Company, class and station, and the fact that a future timetable belongs to the operator, are in the train guide. If the line pauses, the dated note is the train status page. Confirm the day's departure on PeruRail or Inca Rail.

Which months

PROMPERÚ describes two seasons in the Andes. From April to October the days are sunny, the nights are cold and there is little rain. It calls that the better time to visit, with afternoons up to 24 °C and nights that can fall to 0 °C. From November to March the rain is abundant, and the range it gives is 15 °C down to −3 °C. The same page says southern-hemisphere summer, 21 December to 19 March, is the rainy season in the Andes. Clearer weather and the Christmas holidays are not the same months.

The long higher-cap stretch in 2027, 19 June to 2 November, sits inside that drier season. It is also when the citadel is busiest. Busy is not closed. April is the start of the drier season on this calendar. November is the start of the rains. December and January are open, and they are rainy. Part of New Year sits inside the higher cap: 29 to 31 December and 1 to 3 January. A wet-season visit is its own decision. Read Machu Picchu in December and January and, if the arrival is 20 December 2026 to 3 January 2027, the Christmas and New Year page. The countrywide calendar is the best time to visit Peru.

The two 2027 resolutions do not publish a closure of the citadel in February, or in any other month. If the Ministry later announces one, it will be on gob.pe. We will not invent a closure, and we will not plan as if February is shut.

FAQ

Can I buy Machu Picchu tickets for 2027 yet?

No official sale date for 2027 has been published. On 31 October 2026 the Ministry of Culture set the year's daily caps in Resolución Ministerial N.° 408-2026-MC, and in Resolución Ministerial N.° 407-2026-MC it told the Cusco office to configure Tu Boleto before 2027 sales begin. Neither resolution gives the opening day. Check tuboleto.cultura.pe.

Where are official Machu Picchu tickets sold?

The only official online sale site is tuboleto.cultura.pe. Resolución Ministerial N.° 408-2026-MC also allows in-person sale at the authorized offices of the Dirección Desconcentrada de Cultura de Cusco. Each ticket is for one route and one entry hour, in the visitor's name.

How many people can enter per day in 2027?

5,600 visitors a day on 1 to 3 January, 25 to 28 March, 19 June to 2 November, and 29 to 31 December 2027. That is 147 days. Every other day the cap is 4,500. One route can sell out while another route on the same day still has places.

Which circuit should I book?

The official list is three circuits and ten routes. Huayna Picchu is Circuit 3, Route 3-A, not Route 3-B. From 1 January 2027 the alternate-route rules in Resolución Ministerial N.° 407-2026-MC apply, including entry until 10:00 on the mountain routes. We do not guarantee a circuit. We request the route you want as soon as you confirm, subject to availability.

What are the best months in 2027?

PROMPERÚ describes the Andes as drier from April to October, with sunny days and cold nights, and rainy from November to March. The longer higher-cap stretch in 2027, 19 June to 2 November, sits inside that drier season and is also the busy one. December and January are open and rainy.
