Travelling to Cusco and Machu Picchu during Pope Leo XIV's visit (11–16 November 2026)

Photo: Jorge Láscar from Australia / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0. Resized for this page.

By Francisco Arriz Barrantes · TravizPeru · Updated 3 November 2026 · Leer en español

Pope Leo XIV visits Peru from 11 to 16 November, with stops in Lima, Chiclayo, Cusco and Pucallpa. If your trip overlaps, here is what matters.

Cusco airport closes for part of Sunday 15 November

  • Commercial flights are suspended from 7:45 am to 3:05 pm on 15 November (MTC / DGAC). Up to 14 flights are affected.
  • Cusco airport will run 24 hours on 14 and 16 November so airlines can move flights to those days.
  • Check your flight status directly with your airline.

Expect crowds in Cusco and the north

  • The Pope has activities in Sacsayhuamán and Cusco's main square on 15 November.
  • According to Peru's tourism ministry (Mincetur), around 850,000 visitors are expected across the cities he visits, including about 50,000 foreign visitors in Cusco.

Machu Picchu this week

  • A rockfall on 2 November briefly closed the Hiram Bingham bus road; buses are running again.
  • The Machu Picchu–Hidroeléctrica rail section was still interrupted; trains from Ollantaytambo keep running but may change. Check before you go.

How we would plan it

  • Arrive on 14 November or earlier and go straight down to the Sacred Valley. It is lower than Cusco, usually quieter, and easier for acclimatisation.
  • Keep 15 November for the Valley and avoid flights that day.
  • If anyone in your group has a heart or lung condition, ask your doctor before going to high altitude.

Looking ahead to 2027

Peru is promoting the "Route of Leo" in Chiclayo, where the Pope served as bishop (2015–2023). If you would like to see Chiclayo on a later trip, tell us your dates and we will tell you how we can plan it.
