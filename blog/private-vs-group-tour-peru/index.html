Private vs Group Tour in Peru: Which One Fits You?

Photo: Pedro Szekely, CC BY-SA 2.0. Resized for this page.

By Francisco Arriz Barrantes · TravizPeru · Updated 30 October 2026

Both work. Group tours suit travelers who want company and a fixed plan; private tours suit those who want their own pace. Here is an honest comparison without sales talk or invented numbers.

Pace and altitude

A group moves at one speed and follows one route. A private trip can start in the Sacred Valley after landing in Cusco, add a rest morning or slow down if someone feels the altitude — the CDC's gradual-ascent advice is easier to follow when the schedule is yours. See the altitude guide.

Machu Picchu: time slots and circuits

Tickets are personal and sold by circuit and time slot (Ministry of Culture). With a private plan we choose the circuit and hour that suit your mobility and the season. Rules on guides and groups have changed in recent years; we follow the current regulation published at machupicchu.gob.pe. How the official system works is in our tickets guide.

Guides and interests

In a group, the guide addresses everyone. Privately, the guide follows your questions — textiles, archaeology, food, photography.

Hotels, meals and logistics

Private trips let you pick each hotel and keep transfers door to door, with no waiting for others. Groups use set hotels and set times.

When a group tour may be better

Solo travelers who want companions, travelers who enjoy a fixed program, or those who prefer not to make choices. For larger parties of your own, see groups.

FAQ

Is a private tour of Peru worth it?

If pace, flexibility and comfort matter to you, usually yes; if you want company and a fixed plan, a group may suit you better.

Is a private tour better for altitude?

It makes gradual ascent easier, because the itinerary can start lower and adjust day by day.

Do I need a guide at Machu Picchu?

Rules have changed over time; check the current regulation on machupicchu.gob.pe.

Can a private tour be combined with trains like Hiram Bingham?

Yes, see our train guide.

Are private tours only for couples?

No — families, friends and multigenerational groups travel privately too.
