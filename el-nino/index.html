El Niño and your trip to Peru

Photo: Jorge Láscar, CC BY 2.0. Resized for this page.

TravizPeru · Updated 27 October 2026 · Leer en español

In short

  • ENFEN, Peru's official El Niño commission, keeps a Coastal El Niño Alert (Statement No. 18-2026, Oct 16, 2026). It expects ocean warming off Peru to stay at an extraordinary level through January 2027, then ease to strong until early autumn (southern hemisphere) 2027.
  • NOAA (Oct 8, 2026) says a strong-to-very strong El Niño is likely through January–March 2027, and that impacts are more likely "though not guaranteed".
  • According to ENFEN, the main effect in Peru is expected on the north and central coast: above-normal rain and high temperatures.
  • For Cusco, Machu Picchu and the southern highlands, ENFEN expects normal to below-normal rainfall. The usual highland rainy season (roughly December–March) still applies.

What official sources say

  • ENFEN (Oct 16, 2026): Niño 1+2 extraordinary through January 2027, then strong until early autumn 2027; central Pacific (Niño 3.4) very strong through February 2027. Above-normal rain on the north coast, central coast and western northern highlands; normal to below normal in the southern highlands and the Amazon. Above-normal river flows from November in northern rivers and from December in Lima's Rímac and Chillón. Next statement: October 30, 2026.
  • SENAMHI (Oct 22, 2026): within the 2026-2027 Coastal El Niño, rain on the north coast is projected to become heavier and more frequent from November.
  • NOAA CPC (Oct 8, 2026): El Niño Advisory; greater than 83% chance of a strong-to-very strong El Niño through Jan–Mar 2027. Next update: November 12, 2026.

What it means by region

North coast (Tumbes, Piura, Lambayeque, La Libertad)

Where official forecasts expect the most rain, from November 2026 and through the 2027 summer. Roads and services may be affected. Trips in that period need flexibility or an alternative region.

Lima and the central coast

ENFEN expects much higher-than-normal temperatures, above-normal rain and higher flows in the Rímac and Chillón from December. Lima remains the country's gateway.

Cusco, Sacred Valley and Machu Picchu

ENFEN expects normal to below-normal rain and a lower-than-normal Vilcanota river. As in any year, the rainy season can bring occasional changes to roads or trains. As of today, PeruRail's notices do not report rain-related suspensions; see our Machu Picchu train status page.

The Inca Trail network closed for all of February 2026 for its annual maintenance and reopened on March 1, according to SERNANP and Peru's Ministry of Culture. If you plan to hike in February 2027, confirm the official dates once announced.

Lake Titicaca and southern highlands

Normal to below-normal rain and a below-normal lake level, per ENFEN.

Central and southern Amazon

Normal to below-normal rain, per ENFEN.

How we plan

  • Itineraries that can shift days or regions if official forecasts change.
  • We recommend travel insurance and checking what it covers for weather.
  • We follow every ENFEN, SENAMHI and rail operator update.
  • Options such as starting in the Sacred Valley, favoring the south over the north coast, and leaving buffer time before international flights.

We cannot promise weather won't affect a trip; we can plan with you using official information.
