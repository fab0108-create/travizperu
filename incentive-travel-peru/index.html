Machu Picchu with a group

Tickets are bought ahead of time in the official system of the Ministry of Culture (tuboleto.cultura.pe) and are subject to availability. Plan ahead. Whether everyone enters together depends on official quotas and the circuits that are open. The 2026 ticket system is described in this note. The private visit itself is the private Machu Picchu tour. The citadel is on the Machu Picchu page.

Altitude and pace

Cusco sits at 3,399 m. Urubamba, in the Sacred Valley, sits at 2,871 m. Machu Picchu sits at 2,430 m (UNESCO). Many itineraries start in the Sacred Valley. Altitude illness is possible from about 2,400 m, according to a Peruvian state source. Ask your doctor.
