Luxury Private Tours in Peru, Made Entirely Yours

By Francisco Arriz Barrantes · Founder, TravizPeru · APOTUR Certified AP030-11 · Leer en español

A luxury private tour of Peru, in the way I actually build them, is not a nicer seat on someone else's departure. It is your dates, your party, and a route written after I know what you care about.

I'm Francisco Arriz Barrantes, founder of TravizPeru, based in Lima. APOTUR certified, code AP030-11. Twenty-two years in this work. You deal with me, not a desk that rotates staff every season.

What "private" and "luxury" mean here

  • The vehicle and the guide are yours for the days we are in the highlands or the city. You are not waiting for a group to finish the market.
  • The hotel fits the night. Five-star in Lima or Cusco when that is the right room. A lodge in the Sacred Valley when the location matters more than a brand name. I do not lock a hotel into the website before I know your dates.
  • Machu Picchu is handled. The entrance and the train are in the proposal, secured through the official systems, not left as a link for you to fight with.
  • You can reach me while you are in the country. If a train slips or a museum day should become a slow one, we change it.

I do not publish a rate card. The quote matches the nights, the flights, and the trains. I send it within 24 hours of a serious inquiry.

Three shapes I start from

These are on the homepage as starting points, not catalogues. Every one of them says price on request, because that is the truth.

  • Andean Classic, from 5 days. Cusco, the Sacred Valley, and a private Machu Picchu tour. Right when the time is short and you refuse to rush the citadel anyway.
  • Complete Peru, from 10 days. Lima, the Sacred Valley, Machu Picchu, Cusco, and Lake Titicaca, with the internal flights the route needs. The day-by-day version is the tailor-made 10-day itinerary.
  • Grand Expedition, from 15 days. The same country plus the Amazon, at a pace that can hold a rainforest lodge without stealing days from the Andes. Arequipa and Colca Canyon belong on this length when you want them, not on a week that is already full.

How long is actually enough is a separate question. I answered it in how many days you need in Peru.

How the planning works

You tell me the dates, who is coming, and what you want the trip to feel like. Within a day I send a proposal: hotels, the Machu Picchu entry we can still get, trains, and the transfers. You mark it up. We confirm. I do not ask you to trust a PDF that was written for a different month.

People also arrive with a narrower brief. A honeymoon. A trip with children. A single question about whether Peru feels safe enough to book, which I answer directly in the 2026 safety note.
