Best time to visit Machu Picchu

The dry season, May through September, offers the clearest views, the busiest trails, and the highest hotel demand. Shoulder months — April, October, and November — usually give you a strong balance of weather and fewer crowds. Whatever the season, entrance tickets should be secured two to four months ahead. Closer to peak season, some circuits sell out even earlier.

On a private Peru itinerary I usually place Machu Picchu after a night or two on a private Sacred Valley tour, so the altitude and the train timing are settled before the citadel. If you are still deciding the length of the trip, start with how many days in Peru actually fit.

Private Machu Picchu tickets and timing

How early should private Machu Picchu tickets be booked?

Secure the entrance ticket two to four months ahead. In the dry season, May through September, popular circuits and limited add-ons such as Huayna Picchu or Machu Picchu Mountain sell out even earlier. Entries open on a rolling window of about four months. The time slot you want is not something to leave for the gate.

What is included in a luxury Machu Picchu tour with TravizPeru?

A private visit is built around your dates: entrance tickets for a specific circuit and time slot, a private English-speaking guide, a Vistadome or Belmond Hiram Bingham train chosen for your pace, and a hotel in Aguas Calientes or the Sacred Valley. Huayna Picchu or Machu Picchu Mountain can be added when permits are still open.

Vistadome or Hiram Bingham — which train is right?

The right train is the one that fits the day, not a group departure. Vistadome is the scenic train most private visits use. Belmond Hiram Bingham is the choice when the journey itself should feel unhurried. I pair the departure with your hotel and your entrance time.

When is the best time for a private Machu Picchu visit?

May through September is the clearest weather and also the busiest, with the highest hotel demand. April, October, and November usually balance decent weather with fewer people on the trails. Whatever the month, the ticket lead time matters more than chasing a perfect forecast.
