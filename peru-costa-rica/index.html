One private, tailor-made journey: the Peruvian Andes and Inca heritage, then tropical nature in Costa Rica. The length is set for the trip, not taken from a fixed package.

Why combine them

The Andes and Inca heritage — Machu Picchu is a UNESCO World Heritage site — together with tropical nature in Costa Rica.

An example route

The length is tailor-made. One possible order is Lima, then the Sacred Valley and Cusco, then Machu Picchu, then a flight to San José, then regions of Costa Rica chosen for that trip.

  • Lima
  • Sacred Valley and Cusco
  • Private Machu Picchu tour
  • Flight to San José
  • Regions in Costa Rica, chosen for the trip. The Costa Rica page is here.

When to travel

In the Peruvian Andes the rainy season is concentrated roughly November–March, according to the official PROMPERÚ guide to Cusco. In Costa Rica, per the national weather service (IMN), the Pacific side is dry December–March (April is transitional); the Caribbean side has no defined dry season, with February–March and September–October usually least rainy.

The flight connection

Direct flights operate between Lima and San José. Confirm the airline and the current schedule when you book. This page does not treat any airline, duration or frequency as a fixed fact.

Entry requirements

Check Migraciones in Peru and the DGME in Costa Rica for your nationality. This page does not state a visa requirement.
