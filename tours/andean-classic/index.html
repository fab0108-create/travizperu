Andean Classic is the shortest of the three journey styles: from 5 days, price on request. Cusco, the Sacred Valley, and a private Machu Picchu tour. Right when the time is short and you refuse to rush the citadel anyway.

What it includes

  • Cusco & Inca Trail
  • Private Machu Picchu guided tour
  • 5-star hotels
  • Direct WhatsApp support from Francisco, your trip designer

How the days usually run

These are the Andes days of the tailor-made 10-day itinerary. A starting point, not a fixed package.

Day 3

Fly to Cusco, sleep in the valley

The flight is short. The drive down into the Sacred Valley is the point. A light afternoon. No ruin that requires a climb.

Day 4

Sacred Valley

Pisac's terraces, and either the market (Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday) or Chinchero's weavers. Maras and Moray can take the place of one of those, not all of them. The night is still in the valley.

Day 5

Ollantaytambo and the train

Morning at the living Inca town, then PeruRail or Inca Rail to Aguas Calientes. Night by the river so the citadel morning is not a race from Cusco.

Day 6

Machu Picchu

Your guide, the circuit and time we reserved, the bus up from town. Afternoon train back toward the valley or Cusco, unless you want a second night in Aguas Calientes. This day is the private Machu Picchu tour.

Day 7

Cusco, or a slow day

The city if you have the energy. A quiet hotel day if you do not. Altitude is still real at 3,400 meters. I will not fill this day to justify the invoice.
