A ten-day rhythm

This is the day-by-day of the tailor-made 10-day itinerary. A starting rhythm, not a fixed package.

Day 1

Lima, arrival

Private transfer from Jorge Chávez. Miraflores, an early night. Nothing that has to be "achieved."

Day 2

Lima, properly

Huaca Pucllana, Barranco or the historic center, and one meal that was reserved ahead. A well-known dining room if that is the table you want and it is still open. A quieter room if it is not.

Day 3

Fly to Cusco, sleep in the valley

The flight is short. The drive down into the Sacred Valley is the point. A light afternoon. No ruin that requires a climb.

Day 4

Sacred Valley

Pisac's terraces, and either the market (Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday) or Chinchero's weavers. Maras and Moray can take the place of one of those, not all of them. The night is still in the valley.

Day 5

Ollantaytambo and the train

Morning at the living Inca town, then PeruRail or Inca Rail to Aguas Calientes. Night by the river so the citadel morning is not a race from Cusco.

Day 6

Machu Picchu

Your guide, the circuit and time we reserved, the bus up from town. Afternoon train back toward the valley or Cusco, unless you want a second night in Aguas Calientes. This day is the private Machu Picchu tour.

Day 7

Cusco, or a slow day

The city if you have the energy. A quiet hotel day if you do not. Altitude is still real at 3,400 meters. I will not fill this day to justify the invoice.

Day 8

Cusco to Puno

The road takes about 10 hours. We stop on the altiplano when you want the day to be part of the trip, and we drive it straight when you do not. PeruRail's scenic train covers the same journey on selected days and also takes most of the day. It is not a faster option. Both routes cross a pass higher than Cusco.

Day 9

The lake

Uros, then Taquile if the night in Puno was kind. An overnight on Amantani means dropping something else. In ten days, that something is usually this comfortable lodge night, so we choose on purpose.

Day 10

Fly out of Juliaca

About 45 minutes from Puno to Juliaca, then a flight, usually through Lima. If your international connection is the same day, we protect the layover. If it is tight, the honest fix is an extra night in Lima, which is why ten days is a starting length.
