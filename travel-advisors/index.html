Private, tailor-made trips in Peru for luxury travel advisors: bespoke itineraries, private guides, private transfers and on-the-ground support.

What we offer

  • Bespoke private itineraries, written for the traveler's dates and interests.
  • Private guides and private transfers.
  • Trains and Machu Picchu tickets, coordinated with the trip. Tickets are issued through the official system of the Ministry of Culture and depend on availability. The 2026 system is explained in Machu Picchu tickets: the new system.

The private day at the citadel is the private Machu Picchu tour. Wider private journeys are on luxury private tours in Peru.

How we work

  1. You send the traveler profile, the dates and the interests.
  2. We prepare a proposal.
  3. We adjust it with you.
  4. Confirmation, and on-the-ground support for that trip.
